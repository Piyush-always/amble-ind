Why sound helps

Rehab is repetition.
Sound makes it play.

Therapy exercises work through doing the same movement again and again — and that is exactly where motivation drops. Amble Beats answers every movement with sound, so each repetition has a reason.

01

Move

The patient steps, reaches or taps one of the characters.

02

Hear

The system answers instantly with a note or a beat — a clear signal that the movement landed.

03

Repeat

A response every time gives patients, especially children, a reason to try one more.

04

Adjust

The therapist changes distance, order, pace or group size to set the right challenge.

Frequently asked

Questions therapists may have.

Is Amble Beats a medical device?

No. Amble Beats is an interactive music-and-movement system. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. Therapists can choose to use it as an engaging tool within their own sessions.

Does it replace a therapist?

No. The therapist decides the activities, the challenge and whether it is suitable for each person. Amble Beats makes the movements more engaging.

Can it be used seated or by wheelchair users?

The raised drum set places the characters at hand height rather than on the floor. Tell us about your users and your space, and we can discuss placement.

Is it the same product as the school version?

Yes. It is the same Amble Beats system — the activities change, not the hardware.
